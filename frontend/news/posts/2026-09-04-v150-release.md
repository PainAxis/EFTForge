# v1.5.0 - The Weapon Optimizer

**Released:** 2026-09-04

v1.5.0 is bringing the **Optimizer** tool! Instead of building a loadout attachment-by-attachment, tell it what you want (max ergonomics, suppressor, a budget cap, whatever matters to you) and it solves for the best full attachment set in one shot, using the same constraint-solving engine (MILP, HiGHS) that inspired it.

---

## New: The Optimizer

Open it from the new edge-tab next to your build:

![The Optimizer Edge-Tab](./news/images/v150/optimizerTab.webp "The Optimizer Edge-Tab")

- **Weighted priorities** - balance Ergonomics (or EvoErgo), Recoil, and Price against each other with sliders or a triangle control
- **Hard constraints** - budget limit, minimum ergonomics, max weight, minimum magazine capacity, minimum sighting range, max MOA, and a "must include a suppressor" toggle
- **Prevent Overswing** - a toggle that guarantees the result never overswings, no matter what else you ask for
- **Attachment Filtering** - force-include (lock) or ban specific mods by name, then re-solve around them; you can also lock or ban a part straight from the result manifest and it re-optimizes on the spot
- **Weight presets** - save your own slider setups, or reach for a built-in one (Balanced, Min. Operable, Performance, Recoil+, Ergo+)
- **Base vs. Factory Preset costing** - the solver compares buying the base receiver plus attachments against buying the weapon's factory preset outright, and prices whichever route is cheaper; parts that already ship on the factory preset show up in their own **Retained from Preset** group in the results
- Every price respects your current trader loyalty levels, flea market toggle, and player level, same as the rest of the app

![Optimizer Parameters](./news/images/v150/optimizerParameters.webp "Optimizer Parameters")

The constraint-solving approach behind it (MILP, priority weighting, budget/trader-level filtering) is a native reimplementation inspired by **AhaiMk01**'s [Tarkov Weapon Mod Optimizer](https://github.com/AhaiMk01/tarkov-weapon-optimizer), please show the original project some support!

## Also in this update

- Desktop mode's backend startup failures now surface immediately on the splash screen instead of leaving you staring at a stuck loading bar.
- Build preview images now cache with a TTL matched to how often that asset type actually changes, and regenerated images are version-stamped so you always see the latest one instead of a stale cached copy.
- Loading feedback and a re-click guard on gun card selection, so a slow click can't be mistaken for a missed one.
- Toasts now pause their auto-dismiss timer while your mouse is over them.
- Embedded videos now plays muted by default.
