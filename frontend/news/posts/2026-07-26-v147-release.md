# v1.4.7 - Build Tabs, and Moooooore Stats!

**Released:** 2026-07-26

v1.4.7 brings the single largest workflow change since the grid view: **Build Tabs**. Desktop users can now keep multiple guns open at once and switch between them like browser tabs. Alongside it, the hidden stats panel graduates into the **Advanced Stats** panel with three new weapon factors: **Heat, Cooling, and Durability Burn**. **Muzzle Velocity** is now also calculated per build.

---

## New: Build Tabs (Desktop)

Instead of working in a single build session, you can now open as many guns as you want as tabs and jump between them without losing state! These tabs should be very similar to how normal browser tabs work.

![The Build Tabs](./news/images/v147/bTabs.webp "The Build Tabs")

- **One tab per build** - Selecting a gun, loading a saved build, opening a community build, or following a shared link all open in their own tab. Each tab keeps its own attachments, ammo selection, and stats.
- **Smart folding** - If the gun plus its full attachment and ammo state already matches an open tab, that tab is reused instead of spawning an exact duplicate. Right-click Duplicate always creates a new tab if you want one on purpose.
- **Hover previews** - Hovering a tab shows a rich preview with the gun image and a minified stats panel, generated lazily so idle tabs cost nothing. Community builds reuse their pre-rendered card image instead of re-generating one.

![Build Tab Hover Preview](./news/images/v147/bTabsHoverPreview.webp "Build Tab Hover Preview")

- **Right-click menu** - Duplicate, Pin / Unpin, and Close. Pinned tabs are grouped at the front and separated by a divider.
- **Drag to reorder** - Tabs are reordered with a custom mouse-driven drag: siblings slide aside to open a gap at the landing spot, and the bar auto-scrolls when you drag near an overflowing edge.
- **Overflow handling** - The strip scrolls horizontally with eased wheel-to-horizontal scrolling and fading edge indicators, and auto-scrolls a newly created or reactivated tab into view.
- **Marquee labels** - Long gun names scroll on hover instead of being cut off, both on the chips and inside the preview tooltip.
- **Tab counter & warning** - A live tab count sits on the right edge the bar, and passing 20 open tabs raises a one-time performance notice (dismissible for good).

Mobile keeps the existing single-session flow unchanged. Deal with it.

## New Advanced Stats: Heat, Cooling & Durability Burn

The hidden stats panel has been renamed to the **Advanced Stats** panel, and it now carries three new build-wide factors:

- **Heat Factor** - Combined multiplier of every installed mod and the selected ammo's heat buildup per shot.
- **Cooling Factor** - Combined multiplier of every installed mod's cooling rate between shots.
- **Durability Burn** - Combined multiplier of every installed mod and the selected ammo's durability burn per shot.

![Advanced Stats Panel](./news/images/v147/advStats.webp "Advanced Stats Panel")

Per-attachment values are shown in a new **Heat/Cool/Burn** column in the attachment table, and all three stats are wired into the stat change tracker, so shifts between game patches are tracked and highlighted like every other stat.

---

## New Stat: Muzzle Velocity

**Muzzle Velocity** is now calculated and displayed on the stats panel, derived from the selected ammunition's base velocity and every installed attachment that modifies it. With no ammo selected the panel shows a `No Ammo` state instead of a misleading number like in-game (BSG plz fix).

- Attachment velocity modifiers appear as their own column in the attachment table.
- The tracker now watches `velocity_modifier`, displayed as Muzzle Velocity %.

---

## UI Improvements

- **Attachment name column** - Long attachment names are capped again so the stat columns stop getting squeezed, and overflowing names marquee on hover.
- **Attachment table scaling** - The entire attachment table section has been scaled down slightly to accommodate for the new stats.
- **Mobile header** - The header now fades out at its edges when it is horizontally scrollable on narrow viewports.
- **Advanced stats panel** - No longer replays its opening animation when it is re-inserted.

## Mobile

- The profile button and the community build comment form are hidden on mobile. Neither publishing nor commenting is possible there, so the entries were only ever dead ends. Again, deal with it.

## Bug Fixes

- **Flea price cache** - Cached flea prices now persist across browser tabs and sessions instead of being scoped to a single tab, so opening the site in a second tab no longer triggers a fresh fetch and the annoying toasts.
- **My builds search** - Searching your own builds now matches a gun's short name as well as its full name.
- **Localization** - Assorted missing and incorrect translation strings fixed.
