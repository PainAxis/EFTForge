# v1.3.0 - Profile System, Build Comments & Build Tags

**Released:** 2026-05-24

v1.3.0 is a major social update. Three new systems ship together: **User Profiles** with avatars and account settings, **Build Comments** so you can leave feedback on community builds, and **Build Tags** for labelling and filtering builds by playstyle. On top of that, the community builds modal has been heavily polished, and several stats have been cleaned up or recalculated.

---

## User Profiles

I have always been intentional about avoiding traditional account systems for EFTForge. Nobody wants to register yet another account for an obscure website, and centralised credential stores are a liability, they get breached, leaked, etc. At the same time, community features like build sharing naturally benefit from some sense of identity.

The solution is a **quasi-local profile system**: your identity token lives in your browser's local storage, not behind a login wall. There is no password, no email, no registration form. You just open the site and your profile is already there. The **account transfer** flow exists so you can carry that identity to a new device or browser without ever touching a server-side account system.

A new **Profile** button in the top-right navigation opens your user profile modal. From here you can:

- Upload or update your **avatar** (images are resized to 128x128 JPEG and stored server-side)
- Edit your **display name**
- Perform an **account transfer** - relink your previous community builds and comments to your new profile

![The profile dialog](./news/images/v130/profileModal.webp "The profile dialog")

Community builds will now show the author's **avatar and display name** on each card, so you always know who posted what.

## Build Comments

Each community build card now has a **Comments** button. Expanding it shows the existing comments for that build and a text input to post your own. Comments can be deleted by their author or by an admin, so don't be naughty!

![Build comments in action](./news/images/v130/cbComments.webp "Build comments in action")

---

## Build Tags

Now when saving a build, a row of **tag chips** lets you label it with up to 5 preset tags. Things like `Budget`, `Recoil`, `Long Range`, `Meta`, and more. Tags are stored with the build and submitted to the server.

![Build tags in action](./news/images/v130/buildTags.webp "Build tags in action")

A **tag filter row** appears at the top of the community builds list whenever tags are in use. Clicking a tag chip toggles that filter; the row hides itself automatically when no filters are active.

### My Community Builds

The Builds dialog accessed via the site heading bar now has two tabs: **My Local Builds** and **My Community Builds**. Your published builds load lazily in the community tab and can be loaded directly back into the workbench with a **Load** button on each card.

---

## Attachment Favorites

Each row in the attachment table now has a **★ star button**. Clicking it bookmarks that attachment as a favorite, which does two things: favorited attachments always sort to the top of the list, and a **Favorite** toggle in the table header lets you filter down to only your starred items. Favorites are stored in localStorage.

---

## Stats Cleanup

**EvoErgo** and **overswing** have been removed from the site. The graph view's Y axis now defaults to Ergo Modifier. Ergo color indicators have been updated to brighter Material Design colors with bolder weight.

**Arm Stamina** formula has been updated (A new in-house reverse engineered formula!), and the panel gains a Desmos link available for anyone to see the actual full formula. The strength slider is locked to Lv. 51 for now with the new formula.

![The new arm stamina formula](./news/images/v130/armstamFormula.webp "The new arm stamina formula")

The attachment table now uses **base ergo as a secondary sort key** for consistent ordering when primary stats tie.
