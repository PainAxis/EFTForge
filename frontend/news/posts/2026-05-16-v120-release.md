# v1.2.0 - Combo Calculator, Attachment Graph & Build Image Export

**Released:** 2026-05-16

v1.2.0 is the biggest feature release since v1.0.0. Three major systems land in this update: a full **Combo Calculator** that searches thousands of attachment combinations for you, an **Attachment Graph** for visualising any item across two stat axes at once, and a **Build Image Export** that lets you save a clean PNG of your current build. Plus a pile of UX polish throughout.

---

## Combo Calculator

The slot selector now has a **Combo** button alongside the existing List view. Clicking it kicks off a BFS search across all valid attachment combinations for the selected slot and ranks every legal combination by your chosen stat.

![The combo recommender in action](./news/images/v120/comboView.png "The combo recommender in action")

Results stream in live via a progress line that shows which slot is currently being evaluated and how large the search frontier has grown. A hard cap of 10,000 frontier states prevents runaway computation on very dense attachment trees; if that limit is hit, a warning toast lets you know results may be incomplete.

Each combo row shows the full stat delta for the combination: recoil %, ergo delta, EvoErgo delta, weight, price, ₽/Recoil efficiency, and the EE:R ratio (EvoErgo-to-Recoil trade-off, inspiration from [db4tarkov](https://db4tarkov.com) <3). Clicking a row installs the entire combination in one shot.

## Attachment Graph View

A new **Graph** button in the slot selector opens a scatter plot of all attachments for the current slot. Each item appears as its in-game icon with a short-name label, plotted against two configurable axes: choose from V-Recoil, H-Recoil, Ergo, Recoil Modifier, or EvoErgo for each axis.

![The graph view in action](./news/images/v120/graphView.png "The graph view in action")

Key interactions:

- **Ctrl+scroll** to zoom; **middle-drag** to pan; **right-click** or the **reset button** to return to auto-fit
- **Clusters** form when multiple items share the same coordinates, scroll over a cluster to cycle through the items inside it, or use arrow keys while hovering
- The **Graph Properties** panel lets you add any gun or attachment from the entire database to your own custom graph, useful for comparing across weapons or builds
- The site can also automatically generate & export your graph via the **Export Graph** button at 4x resolution with item images inlined:
![Site-generated graph](./news/images/v120/graphGenerated.png "Site-generated graph")

An **Export** button has been added to the Workbench panel toolbar. Clicking it renders the current build to a PNG available to be saved & exported:

## Toast & Announcement Improvements

- Toasts that are clickable-to-dismiss now show a subtle `x` hint on hover; non-dismissible toasts block the click entirely
- Export toasts (build image and graph) update their message live via a new `setToastStatus` helper
- Indefinite "..." loading toasts now show animated pulsing dots
