# v1.5.8 - PvP Season Pricing, EvoErgo Curves & Filter Presets

**Released:** 2026-09-16

This update adds a full PvP Season pricing mode to the Optimizer and price panel, extends the Weapon Optimizer with EvoErgo tradeoff curves and an Ergonomics min/max range constraint, and lets you save custom attachment filter presets. Plus a batch of reliability and performance fixes!

---

## New: PvP Season Pricing

The price panel and Optimizer now support three game modes: **PvE**, **PvP**, and **PvP Season**, each with its own flea market prices.

![The new game mode toggle](./news/images/v158/gamemodeToggle.webp "The new game mode toggle")

- Pick a game mode from the Optimizer's Flea Market & Traders panel, budget limits and price-based objectives all solve against that mode's prices.
- The build cost panel's PvE/PvP toggle now includes PvP Season alongside the existing modes.

---

## Optimizer Upgrades!

- **EvoErgo tradeoff curves (BETA)** - the Weapon Optimizer can now plot EvoErgo vs. Recoil or EvoErgo vs. Price. Because of how EvoErgo is calculated and traded off, solving takes longer, and at the highest sampling resolution on attachment-heavy weapons it may not finish within the 30s solve limit.
- **Ergonomics Range replaces Min Ergonomics** - you can now set both a floor and a ceiling on base Ergonomics instead of just a minimum.
- **Clearer failure reasons** - when the optimizer can't find a build, it now tells you which specific setting is the problem (budget, ergonomics range, magazine capacity, and so on).
- Per-axis solves now tiebreak on price, so the optimizer won't hand you the red ARE buffer tube anymore unless you've locked it in yourself (throwback to my own demo video on the new optimizer).
- The optimizer no longer has to chase a build that matches the Ergonomics cap exactly - the solver now also searches a small margin around the cap for a better one. To explain further: before this update, base-Ergonomics solves had a hardcoded 100 cap, and the solver would always try to find a build with Ergonomics exactly equal to 100, no matter how terrible its recoil was. Now it also checks around that cap for a build with more reasonable recoil and price (for example, a 99.5 Ergonomics build with noticeably lower recoil than the one sitting at exactly 100).

## Attachment Filter Presets & Persisted Settings

- Save your locked/banned attachment selections as a named preset and reload them anytime.
- Optimizer settings (weights, constraints, filters) now persist per weapon.
- The locked/banned attachment list is redesigned as icon tiles.
- Axion Kobra sight shade and NSPU-M night scope are now banned by default for all applicable weapons (you can still manually remove the bans on them).

![Attachment filter presets](./news/images/v158/attachmentFilters.webp "Attachment filter presets")

## Also in this update:

- Build preview image generation is now off by default. Turning it on requires reading a **very** short disclaimer first.
- Rewrote the preview image generation queue, it should(?) be faster now under load.
- Build tab hover tooltip previews now correctly reflect preview image generation state live.
- Optimizer solves now cancel immediately when you navigate away or disconnect, instead of continuing to run on the server backend.
- Fixed the optimizer's elapsed timer starting before the solve stream actually began.
